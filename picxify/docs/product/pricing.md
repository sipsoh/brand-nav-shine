# Pricing

Viewers are free; creators/editors pay. Public UI speaks in "dashboards per month";
internally usage is metered in credits (see SETUP.md §16).

| Plan | Price | Included |
|---|---:|---|
| Free | $0 | 3 dashboards/month, small uploads, public share links with watermark |
| Creator | $19/mo annual ($24 monthly) | 50 dashboards/month, private share links, exports, editable assumptions, unlimited viewers |
| Team | $49/editor/mo annual ($59 monthly) | shared workspace, brand themes, comments, permissions, 300 generations/editor/month |
| Business | from $299/mo | 3 editors included, white label, custom domain, retention controls, API, scheduled refresh |

Enforced limits: upload size, rows profiled synchronously, generations/month, private share
links, brand kits, team members, refresh frequency, retention duration.
