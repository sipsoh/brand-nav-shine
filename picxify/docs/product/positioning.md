# Positioning

**Picxify: Drop in messy data. Get the story.**

Picxify turns messy business data into polished, trustworthy, client-ready dashboards.
It is not "a chatbot that makes charts" — it packages cleaning, profiling, story detection,
template-first dashboard generation, source traces, and one-click sharing into a single
workflow that works without prompting.

Primary wedge: **client-ready reports** for marketing agencies, consultants, founders,
operators, and customer success teams.
